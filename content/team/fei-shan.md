## Professional background

Fei Shan has designed and built software systems professionally since 2006,
working across distributed systems architecture, machine learning, and
data-driven platforms.

As **Chief Technology Officer of BrandBridge** (Shanghai, 2016–2019), he held
full ownership of end-to-end system architecture and technical strategy, and
independently designed and evolved the company's core platform architecture —
combining technical depth with the commercial and organizational
responsibilities of a technology executive.

In staff-level engineering roles, he contributed to an Azure-based
microservices platform serving T-Mobile at **Capgemini**, and to a
cloud-native Micro ERP App Store platform at **Chanjet Information
Technology**. In 2026 he founded **AlphaStack LLC** to build infrastructure
for AI-native businesses, and he leads the Lab's research program.

## Why this research program

Two decades of building and operating distributed platforms — from
service-oriented architecture and concurrent systems to cloud-native
microservices and LLM-based applications — led to a consistent observation:
the hardest problems in production AI are not model quality, but state,
coordination, failure handling, and verifiability across distributed agents.
The Lab's four research areas formalize that observation into an
investigable research program.
